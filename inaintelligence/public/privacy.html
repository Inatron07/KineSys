Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This policy describes how KineSys ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information through our real estate services and the InaIntelligence platform, including our WhatsApp Business messaging.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide to us or our brokers — name, phone number, email address, and property preferences — when you inquire about a property, request a viewing, or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Messages exchanged with us over WhatsApp, including automated replies from our assistant, which are stored to maintain a record of the conversation and follow up appropriately.
  • Basic account and activity information for our internal team members who use the platform to manage leads and listings.

How we use this information

  • To respond to inquiries, share property information, and arrange site visits.
  • To route your inquiry to the right broker on our team.
  • To send you outreach or follow-up messages about properties that may interest you, where you've engaged with us or consented to be contacted.
  • To improve our internal tools and keep accurate records of client communications.

WhatsApp messaging

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (operated by Meta) to communicate with prospective clients. Messages you send us may be processed by an AI assistant to provide faster responses using our current property listings; a member of our team can take over any conversation at any time. Standard WhatsApp and Meta data-handling terms also apply to messages sent through this channel.

Data storage and sharing

Your information is stored securely in our database and is not sold to third parties. We share information only with service providers that help us operate — such as Meta (for WhatsApp messaging) and our hosting and AI providers — solely to the extent necessary to provide our services to you.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, correct your information, or delete your data at any time by messaging us directly or contacting us using the details below.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at inatron@kinesys.net.
